On Tuesday, May 14, 2024, Avik Ghosh will be presenting our poster, "FishSense Lite: An In-situ Camera and Laser-based Fish Length Measurement System," at the [Southern California Coastal Ocean Observing System (SCCOOS) poster session](https://sccoos.org/poster-session/), from 4:00 to 6:00 PM. The poster covers how a diver with a standard underwater camera, a dive laser, and a 3D-printed mount can measure fish without catching them, along with the length data we've collected with [REEF](https://www.reef.org/) volunteers in the Florida Keys and Baja California. It also previews what's next: a sturdier aluminum mount and software that can keep up with more divers. If you're there, come find us.

*Authors: Avik Ghosh, Christopher L. Crutchfield, Kyle Hu, Jen Loch, Alli Candelmo, Brice Semmens, Curt Schurgers, and Ryan Kastner.*
